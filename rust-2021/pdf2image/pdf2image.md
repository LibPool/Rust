# pdf2image

> 标签: Rust

## 简介

A simplified port of Python's pdf2image that wraps pdftoppm and pdftocairo to convert PDFs into images.

## 官网

- 官网：https://github.com/styrowolf/pdf2image
- crates.io 页面：https://crates.io/crates/pdf2image
