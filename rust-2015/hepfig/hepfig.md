# hepfig

> 标签: Rust

## 简介

Publication figures for HEP fits without ROOT: CascadesCombine-style stack, significance, 2D yield, impacts, limit and z-score figures, from CascadesCombine runs, Combine files, cardfit or cascades_yields outputs

## 官网

- 源码仓库：https://gitlab.cern.ch/dgrove/hepfig
