# automotive_diag

> 标签: Rust

## 简介

Unified Diagnostic Services/UDS (ISO-14229-1), KWP2000 (ISO-142330), OBD-II (ISO-9141), and DoIP (ISO-13400) definitions to communicate with the road vehicle ECUs in Rust.
