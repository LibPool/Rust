# rustdct

> 标签: Rust

## 简介

Compute Discrete Cosine Transforms (DCT) of any size in O(nlogn) time, in pure Rust.

## 官网

- crates.io 页面：https://crates.io/crates/rustdct
- 文档：https://docs.rs/rustdct/
- 源码仓库：https://github.com/ejmahler/rust_dct
