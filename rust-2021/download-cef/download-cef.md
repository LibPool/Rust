# download-cef

> 标签: Rust

## 简介

Download and extract pre-built CEF (Chromium Embedded Framework) archives.

## 官网

- crates.io 页面：https://crates.io/crates/download-cef
- 源码仓库：https://github.com/tauri-apps/cef-rs
